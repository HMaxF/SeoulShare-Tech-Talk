What is an LLM?

  • LLM = Large Language Model
  • An AI system trained on massive amounts of text to understand and generate human-like language
  • It can answer questions, write text, translate, and more

History of LLM

  • Before 2017: Rule-based & simple machine learning (limited)
  • 2017: Transformer architecture (breakthrough)
  • 2020: GPT-3 shows strong language ability
    * GPT = Generative Pre-trained Transformer
  • 2022: ChatGPT becomes first widely used public AI

Notes:

  • All GPT models are LLMs, But not all LLMs are GPT.
  • "All Hondas are cars, but not all cars are Hondas."

What are the LLM examples?

  • ChatGPT by OpenAI
  • Gemini by Google
  • Claude by Anthropic
  • LLaMa by Meta
  • Copilot by Microsoft
  • etc.

How LLM Works?

  1. Trained on massive data
    • Billions of sentences from books, websites, conversations
    • Learns patterns of how humans write and speak
  2. Understands your input (prompt)
    • LLM does NOT truly understand like humans
    • It recognizes patterns and predicts what comes next
    • Examples:
      • “What is the capital of France?” → “Paris”
      • “Write an email…” → generates email format
  3. Breaks text into tokens
    Token = small piece of text:
    • A word
    • Part of a word
    • Punctuation
    Examples:
    • "Today is Sunday!" → ["Today", "is", "Sunday", "!"]
    • "playing" → ["play", "ing"]
    • "unbelievable" → ["un", "believ", "able"]
  4. Converts tokens into numbers (the “magic”)
    • ["Today", "is", "Sunday", "!"] → [100213, 23421, 5667561, 50]
    • AI understands numbers, not words
    • Finds patterns using these numbers
  5. Predicts the next word
    • Based on probability from training data
    • Builds sentence one word at a time
  6. Remembers conversation (Context Window)
    • Stores recent conversation (e.g. ~128K tokens)
    • Helps maintain context and continuity
    • Important for better answers and fewer mistakes ("hallucinations")

💡 Simple analogy:
“LLM is like a super-smart autocomplete trained on the entire internet.”

What is LLM Hallucination?

Hallucination = when AI gives a confident but incorrect answer.

  • AI may invent facts
  • AI may give outdated or incomplete information
  • The answer sounds correct, but is actually wrong

Example:

Asking for a book reference → AI may generate a book title or author that does not exist

💡 LLMs are designed to always generate a response, even when they are uncertain or no right answer.

Lesson: Always verify important information ✅

How to Build an LLM?

Building an LLM is complex and requires huge resources:

  1. Collect massive text data (books, websites, code)
  2. Train a neural network (millions to billions of parameters)
  3. Use powerful GPUs (weeks/months of training)
  4. Fine-tune with human feedback

Reality: This costs millions of dollars 😄

Good news: You don’t need to build one—you can download and run one locally!

What are the applications of LLM?

  • Copywriting
    Generate emails, ads, blog posts, product descriptions
  • Chatbot (Knowledge Base)
    Answer company FAQs, customer support, internal docs
  • Code Generation
    Generate code, SQL queries, scripts, websites
  • Text Generation
    Summarize, translate, write stories, create content

  • Personal AI assistants
  • More private (local AI)

    👉 Beginner-friendly, works on low-end PC

What models for low RAM (no GPU)?

Good models for normal laptops:

  • Phi-3 → very small, fast
  • Gemma → lightweight, stable
  • Mistral (7B) → good balance
  • LLAMA3.3-8B-Instruct Thinking Heretic Uncensored Claude 4.5 Opus High Reasoning

Minimum requirement:

  • 8GB RAM → OK
  • 16GB RAM → Better experience

💡 Tips:

  • Choose “4-bit quantized models” for faster performance.
  • In LMStudio, after installed and running, open the "Model Search" to search and download your selected model.
